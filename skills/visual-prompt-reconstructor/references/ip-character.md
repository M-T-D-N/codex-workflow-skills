# IP Character

Use this reference for mascots, chibi or Q-version characters, trend toys, blind-box figures, stylized creatures, and any request that needs recurring character identity.

## Observe identity

- Overall silhouette and the one or two shapes that make it recognizable at thumbnail size.
- Head-to-body ratio, torso and limb proportions, hand and foot scale, and center of gravity.
- Face layout: eye spacing and shape, brows, nose, mouth, cheeks, ears, and expression system.
- Hair or head form, front/side silhouette, color blocks, and distinctive tufts or ornaments.
- Clothing layers, seams, closures, patterns, footwear, props, and accessories.
- Fixed palette and the exact placement of identity colors.
- Surface impression: illustration marks or 3D material such as matte PVC, resin, ceramic, clay, fabric, metal, or transparent parts.
- Pose, gesture, emotional tone, viewing angle, base, packaging, and background.

## Character lock

For same-character or series work, separate fixed identity from scene variables:

- **Locked:** silhouette, proportions, face-element positions, hairstyle/head form, signature clothing and accessories, fixed palette, and material language.
- **Variable:** pose, expression within the established range, camera, background, and non-signature props.
- **Avoid:** altered face layout, missing signature parts, changed costume structure, palette drift, inconsistent material, or unintended anatomy duplication.

A single view cannot prove hidden sides or guarantee perfect identity consistency. Mark unseen details as unspecified, or ask for additional views when those details are necessary.

## Compile

Start with the locked identity before pose, scene, and finish. For blind-box or trend-toy renders, specify scale cues, molded construction, seams, base contact, surface roughness, soft studio light, and background only when visible or requested.

Use an IP or brand name only when provided by the user or clearly established by the image. Describe uncertain characters by visible traits rather than assigning an identity.
